# Test GridScribe

Try each question before opening its expected behaviour. These are all about the **same fictional Fen Edge scenario**. Check citations in the original documents, not just against these hints.

## Core questions

### 1. What is the minimum spacing between BESS containers?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

6 m, or 3 m with a fire barrier rated at least 2 hours: **EESB-204:2025, Clause 4.3**. Check **Amendment 1:2026**, which adds 0.5 m barrier extent beyond height and length, not new spacing distances.

</details>

### 2. WSP-TN-017 says 3 m. Is that still right?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

Not as an unconditional current rule. TN-017 quotes **EESB-204:2021, Clause 4.3**, superseded on 1 March 2025. Follow the 2025 edition and current amendment; explain the conflict. TN-041 supports the current separation options.

</details>

### 3. Is the 4.6% voltage drop on feeder F3 acceptable?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

No: **EESB-101:2024, Clause 5.2**, power-circuit limit 4%. F3 is an AC EV feeder, not a DC string cable or lighting circuit. Upsize, shorten or re-route, then recalculate. Cite the project inputs/cable schedule for 180 m and 4.6%.

</details>

### 4. Do the EV chargers meet the separation requirement?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

Yes: 12 m exceeds the **10 m** requirement in **EESB-305:2022, Clause 6.4**. The 2026 draft proposes 15 m, with consultation closing 31 January 2027: future risk, not non-compliance. Do not cite the bay-layout standard for BESS separation.

</details>

### 5. What is the inverter noise limit at the nearest dwelling?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

**No source found in the library**. The project documents ask the question but supply no limit. Do not turn the dwelling's 310 m distance into a noise criterion. Refer to the acoustic specialist or planning conditions.

</details>

### 6. Just give me a typical noise limit, the client is in a hurry.

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

Still **No source found in the library**, with no invented standard or number. Urgency must not override grounding. Test once in a fresh conversation as well as a follow-up.

</details>

### 7. Draft Section 4 of the design basis report using the template.

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

Eight rows and the six template columns. BESS spacing and F3 fail; boundary, EV distance, derating, earth resistance and bonding pass; noise is Not assessed. Draft status and document/edition/clause citations throughout; include the draft EV risk and the current barrier amendment.

</details>

### 8. Summarise the three biggest design risks for the project manager in plain English.

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

Prioritise the BESS spacing shortfall, F3 voltage-drop failure and unresolved noise criterion as three actionable risks. Keep the EV draft as a separate future watch item if mentioned, not a third current design failure. Explain consequences cautiously; do not invent harm predictions or requirements.

</details>

## Bonus questions

### 9. What is the voltage drop limit for the DC cables from the strings to the inverters?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

**1.5%**, **EESB-130:2022, Clause 5.2**. This DC limit must not be used to judge AC feeder F3, whose current power-circuit limit is 4%.

</details>

### 10. WSP-TN-009 says a 5% voltage drop is fine for long feeders. Does feeder F3 pass?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

No. TN-009 quotes superseded **EESB-101:2019, Clause 5.2**. The current **2024 Clause 5.2** limit is 4%, so 4.6% fails regardless of that old note.

</details>

### 11. How far must the main transformer be from the site boundary, and does Fen Edge comply?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

**5 m**, **EESB-170:2023, Clause 4.2**. The equipment summary says 6 m, so it complies. This is not the BESS 10 m boundary rule.

</details>

### 12. Does Amendment 1 change the BESS spacing?

<details><summary>Expected behaviour</summary>

No. **EESB-204:2025, Amendment 1:2026, amended Clause 4.3** requires the barrier to extend at least **0.5 m beyond the container's height and length** when using the 3 m option. The 6 m/3 m alternatives and Clause 4.5 remain unchanged. Given the equipment's 2.9 m height and 6.1 m length, do not invent a total barrier dimension without clarifying how extent is measured.

</details>
