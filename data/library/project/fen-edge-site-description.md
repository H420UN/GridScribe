## 1. Location and extent

Fen Edge Energy Hub occupies a **62 ha site near the fictional village of Wexley Fen, Cambridgeshire**. The client is Fenland Power Co-operative. The site description supports the electrical design basis review by explaining where the principal equipment zones sit within the development.

The project is fictional, including its village and client. This narrative is not a geographical survey or a record of an actual planning application.

## 2. Solar-array zone

The solar-array zone provides the principal land use for the **30 MW solar farm**. The array area is distinct from the electrical equipment compound and the public charging area. Array circuit information belongs to the relevant electrical design records rather than being inferred from the overall site area.

For reporting, use “solar-array zone” consistently rather than treating the entire site as a single equipment platform.

## 3. BESS compound

The BESS compound accommodates **four 5 MWh containers**. The detailed arrangement is recorded in the design inputs: **4 m between containers, no fire barrier, and 12 m from the site boundary**.

These are three separate descriptions of the arrangement. The compound outline should not be mistaken for an individual container envelope, and the internal equipment separation should not be read as a perimeter clearance.

## 4. Inverter-room zone

The inverter-room zone accommodates the project's **two central inverters**. It is an equipment space separate from the BESS containers. The room is uncooled; the electrical design inputs record the cable ambient assumption used for its calculations.

The location description does not replace the room equipment schedule or the circuit-specific cable record. Those documents should retain their own identifiers when referenced in the report.

## 5. EV hub and public car park

The **six-bay EV rapid-charging hub sits beside the public car park**. Its nearest charger is **12 m from a BESS container**. The public parking function and the charging equipment should remain distinguishable in layout discussions.

The supply feeder to this area is identified as F3 in the cable schedule. A route serving the hub is not necessarily a straight line between the equipment zones.

## 6. Substation and access

The site includes a **33 kV substation** and the main **33/0.4 kV transformer**, which is **6 m from the southern site boundary**. The transformer location is a separate equipment relationship from the BESS boundary clearance.

The access road links the entrance with the equipment zones and supports site circulation. Internal walkways and access routes are coordinated with the equipment arrangement, including the orientation of BESS vent discharge.

## 7. Surrounding reference point

The nearest dwelling is **310 m north-east of the site**. This reference provides geographical context for the project pack.

## 8. Use in the report

Use this description for location and layout context. Use the design inputs, schedules and test records for detailed electrical values, and the relevant current standards for acceptance requirements. Do not substitute a general zone description for a measured equipment relationship.
