## 1. Project identity

Fen Edge Energy Hub is a fictional development for **Fenland Power Co-operative**, in Cambridgeshire. The scheme comprises a **30 MW solar farm**, four battery energy storage containers and a six-bay EV rapid-charging hub. These inputs describe the arrangement to be reviewed; they are not a statement that every design decision already complies.

The site description, equipment summary, cable schedule and pre-commissioning results provide supporting detail. Use the same project identity throughout the report so that historic case-study information is not mistaken for Fen Edge data.

## 2. BESS arrangement

There are **four BESS containers, each rated 5 MWh**. The design has **4 m between containers** and **no fire barrier**. The BESS containers are **12 m from the site boundary**.

The internal separation and the boundary distance are separate inputs. Do not treat the boundary clearance as the distance between containers. No barrier option has been incorporated into this recorded arrangement; a possible later option should remain distinct from the present design value.

## 3. EV charging hub

The hub has **six rapid chargers**. The nearest rapid charger is **12 m from a BESS container**. This is the nearest equipment relationship, not a parking-bay dimension. The equipment summary identifies the charger rating and the hub's relationship to the public car park.

The client has asked for discussion of future flexibility, as recorded in the meeting minutes. That request does not change the stated 12 m design distance.

## 4. Feeder F3

**F3 runs from the main LV board to the EV hub.** Its route length is **180 m**, and its calculated origin-to-load AC voltage drop is **4.6%**.

These are the current recorded calculation inputs and result. Any proposed cable or routing change should be described as an action, not silently substituted into the report as though it had already been adopted. The cable schedule repeats the same F3 value alongside other site circuits.

## 5. Inverter-room cable basis

The inverter room is **uncooled**. The cable design ambient is **40 °C**, and a **0.87 derating factor has been applied**. These figures describe the cable assessment basis, not battery-cell temperature or a room temperature measured during testing.

## 6. Earthing evidence

The earth electrode test result is **3.8 Ω**. Each BESS container is bonded to the main earth ring at **two separate points**. The October pre-commissioning record identifies the earth measurement method and the associated electrical test summary.

## 7. Client question

The client asks WSP to **“confirm the inverter noise limit at the nearest dwelling (310 m away)”**.
