## 1. Meeting details

The meeting reviewed the fictional Fen Edge Energy Hub for Fenland Power Co-operative. Participants attended by role: Client Project Sponsor, Client Operations Representative, WSP Electrical Design Lead and WSP Project Manager. These minutes record the discussion and actions; they are not a standards approval or a signed design acceptance.

The project remains a 30 MW solar farm with four 5 MWh BESS containers and six rapid chargers in Cambridgeshire.

## 2. BESS land use

The Client Project Sponsor asked to **retain the 4 m spacing between BESS containers to save land**. The current design has **no fire barrier**. The team recorded the preference without treating it as permission to depart from the applicable standard.

The Electrical Design Lead will set out the current requirement and practical options in the report. Any change selected after review will need to be reflected in the design inputs rather than assumed to have happened during this meeting.

## 3. Boundary and equipment context

The review retained the recorded **12 m BESS-to-boundary distance**. This is separate from the internal spacing discussed above. The main transformer is **6 m from the southern boundary**, as shown in the equipment summary.

The minutes do not combine those equipment relationships into a single generic site clearance.

## 4. EV future flexibility

The client asked whether the EV hub is **“future-proof” for the draft standard**. The current nearest charger-to-BESS separation remains **12 m**.

The Electrical Design Lead will distinguish the current published requirement from the consultation proposal. The client asked for a clear explanation of any low-cost opportunity to reserve space, not an assumption that every draft proposal is already mandatory.

## 5. Feeder and evidence review

The team noted F3 as the **180 m main LV board-to-EV hub feeder with 4.6% calculated voltage drop**. WSP will compare the recorded value with the current standard and describe any corrective option separately from the existing design.

The report will also carry through the **40 °C inverter-room cable ambient, applied 0.87 factor, 3.8 Ω earth result and two-point BESS bonding**. These values should remain consistent with the supporting schedules and test summary.
