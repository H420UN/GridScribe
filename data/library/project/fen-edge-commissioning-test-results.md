## 1. Record purpose

This summary supports the electrical design basis review for the fictional Fen Edge Energy Hub, Cambridgeshire, for Fenland Power Co-operative. The results are **pre-commissioning results from October 2026**. They should not be relabelled as a final operational acceptance certificate.

The extract presents selected electrical evidence relevant to the report. Its scope is not an assertion that unlisted tests have failed or that every other project activity is absent.

## 2. Earth electrode measurement

The earth electrode resistance was **3.8 Ω**, measured by the **fall-of-potential method**. The measured value matches the earth result in the project design inputs.

| Item | Recorded result or basis |

The acceptance maximum is **10 Ω** under EESB-150:2023, Clause 4.2. The recorded result is compliant. The measurement method and acceptance value should retain their separate source references in the report.

## 3. LV insulation results

The **lowest recorded insulation resistance was 45 MΩ at 500 V DC** across the LV circuits included in this pre-commissioning test set.

EESB-190:2024, Clause 8.1, requires at least **1 MΩ at 500 V DC**. The lowest recorded result therefore satisfies that criterion. Preserve the test voltage when summarising the result; quoting only a resistance omits part of the comparison basis.

This is an insulation result, not a cable voltage-drop result. It does not change the F3 calculation in the cable schedule.

## 4. Container-bond inspection

The inspection summary records **two separate bonds from each of the four BESS containers to the main earth ring**. This supports the project input describing two-point bonding and the requirement in EESB-150:2023, Clause 4.4.

The bond provision and electrode resistance are separate observations. Do not infer the number of bonds solely from the resistance result, or describe the resistance as measured separately for every container when this summary reports the site electrode arrangement.

## 5. Commissioning interface

The commissioning plan includes the client's representative as witness for BESS commissioning in accordance with EESB-190:2024, Clause 9.2. This pre-commissioning extract does not replace that event's witness record and makes no claim that final commissioning has already occurred.

## 6. Report use

For the resistance row, record 3.8 Ω as the project value and cite EESB-150:2023, Clause 4.2, for the acceptance maximum. Cite this project document as evidence of the result, not as the source of the standard's limit.

## 7. Review boundary

Acceptable test results do not resolve a separate equipment-siting or cable-calculation issue. Keep the report's conclusions limited to what each result actually demonstrates and preserve the distinction between measured values, observed connections and proposed future changes.
