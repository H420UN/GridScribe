# EV charging hubs

| Title block | Detail |
|---|---|
| Document number | EESB-305 |
| Edition | 2022 |
| Status | Current |
| Date | 1 November 2022 |
| Scope | Rapid-charger interfaces with BESS equipment and visibility of emergency stops |

## 1. Application

This fictional document addresses the arrangement of rapid chargers at mixed energy sites. It remains the current edition while the proposed 2026 replacement is under consultation. A published consultation document is not automatically an instruction to redesign a compliant installation to the proposed value.

## 2. Design boundary

The charging hub should be identifiable on the site plan, together with nearby BESS containers and the individual charging bays. The record should distinguish the charger equipment from parking markings. A design review should use the relevant equipment relationship rather than assuming that a bay dimension represents the charger-to-container distance.

## 3. Interface information

The layout review should use consistent equipment identifiers across drawings, schedules and the design basis report. Where several chargers or containers are present, identifying the nearest relationship makes the stated minimum separation understandable. A general description such as “remote from storage” does not provide a traceable design value.

## 4. Relationship to other documents

Bay size, accessible-bay width and signage are addressed by EESB-320:2025. Those provisions do not replace the separation requirement in this document. Electrical feeder voltage drop is a separate matter under EESB-101:2024. A compliant charger location does not establish that its supply feeder is correctly sized.

## 5. Review record

The review should preserve the design value and the requirement in separate fields. This allows a reader to understand the conclusion without reconstructing the geometry from a verbal description. If a future layout reservation is discussed, identify it as an option rather than silently changing the current design distance.

## 6.4. Rapid chargers near BESS

Rapid chargers shall be at least **10 m from any BESS container**. Use this clause as the current charger-to-BESS separation requirement. The distance is not the same subject as BESS-to-BESS separation or BESS-to-boundary clearance.

The nearest charger-to-container relationship should be recorded in the layout assessment. Where that minimum relationship satisfies the requirement, larger separations elsewhere should not be reported as additional deficiencies.

## 6.6. Emergency-stop visibility

An emergency stop shall be **within sight of every charging bay**. The design record should identify which stop is visible from each bay. The existence of an emergency stop somewhere within the hub does not, by itself, demonstrate the required visibility relationship for every bay.

## 7. Consultation interface

EESB-305:2026 DRAFT proposes a 15 m separation in its proposed Clause 6.4. Consultation closes on 31 January 2027, and that proposal is not in force. It may be discussed as a future design risk or an opportunity to preserve flexibility. It shall not replace the current 10 m requirement in an acceptance statement.

## 8. Reporting

Reference the 2022 edition and the appropriate clause for each current conclusion. Keep any discussion of the draft in a separate advisory statement so that a reader does not mistake a future possibility for a present non-compliance.
