# EV charging bay layout and signage

| Title block | Detail |
|---|---|
| Document number | EESB-320 |
| Edition | 2025 |
| Status | Current |
| Date | 1 July 2025 |
| Scope | Charging-bay dimensions, accessible-bay width and information signs |

## 1. Application

This fictional standard addresses the marked spaces and user information at an EV charging hub. It contains **no separation distances to BESS containers**. A document title mentioning EV charging does not make every clause in that document applicable to every charging-related question.

## 2. Layout information

The layout record should identify each charging bay and distinguish the marked parking space from the charger equipment position. Use consistent bay identifiers in drawings and equipment schedules. Where an accessible bay is provided, identify it explicitly rather than asking the reviewer to infer its function solely from a wider rectangle.

The drawing should make the bay dimensions readable without relying on a general statement that the hub follows a standard arrangement.

## 3.2. Standard bay dimensions

Each charging bay shall be at least **2.5 m × 5.0 m**. The dimensions apply to the bay, not the equipment enclosure. Record the width and length clearly on the layout or in an associated bay schedule.

A minimum dimension is not a mandated exact size. A bay exceeding the stated minimum should not be marked deficient because it is not identical to the tabulated figure.

## 3.5. Accessible bay

At least **one accessible bay shall be 3.6 m wide**. Identify the accessible bay in the layout record. The general bay minimum in Clause 3.2 should not be substituted for this accessible-bay width.

The record should distinguish the accessible provision from other bays, while keeping the same bay identifiers in the user information schedule and the layout. This makes the intended arrangement understandable to reviewers working from either document.

## 4. Equipment and bay relationship

The design description should show which charger serves which bay. A change to charger allocation should be reflected in the bay and sign schedules. This document does not set charger-to-storage separation, electrical feeder voltage drop or residual current protection.

Those interfaces should be reviewed under their own current sources rather than inferred from the fact that the bay markings satisfy this standard.

## 5.1. User information

Signage shall identify the **tariff, emergency contact and emergency-stop location**. The sign schedule should show how that information is associated with the charging hub. A sign displaying a tariff alone does not demonstrate all the information listed in this clause.

This clause concerns information about the stop location. The visibility of an actual emergency stop from every bay is addressed separately by EESB-305:2022, Clause 6.6.

## 6. Reporting boundaries

For charger-to-BESS separation, use EESB-305:2022, Clause 6.4, not this document. The 2026 consultation draft should remain separately identified as a proposal. A compliance statement should name the subject being assessed and cite the source that actually contains its requirement, even when another document has a closely related title.
