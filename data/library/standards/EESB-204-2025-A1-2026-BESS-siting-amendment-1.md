# BESS siting — Amendment 1

| Title block | Detail |
|---|---|
| Document number | EESB-204:2025 A1 |
| Edition | 2026 |
| Status | Current amendment |
| Date | Issued 1 June 2026 |
| Scope | Amendment to EESB-204:2025 Clause 4.3 only; barrier extent for the reduced-spacing option |

## 1. Amendment purpose

This amendment adds a geometric provision to the fire-barrier option in the current BESS siting standard. It is not a new complete edition of EESB-204. Read it with EESB-204:2025 so that the original separation choices and the additional barrier detail are understood together.

The amendment does not revive any provision of the superseded 2021 edition. A citation to an amendment should identify the base edition that it modifies, rather than suggesting that a short amendment contains the entire siting standard.

## 2. Extent of change

**Only Clause 4.3 is amended.** The 6 m unbarriered separation and the 3 m barrier option remain unchanged. The requirement for the barrier to be rated for at least 2 hours also remains part of the base clause.

The amendment does not change Clause 4.5 or its 10 m minimum BESS-to-boundary clearance. It does not alter gas detection under Clause 5.1. A report should not attribute a new boundary distance or a different detection requirement to this document.

## 3. Application to the barrier option

The additional provision applies where the 3 m option is used with a fire barrier. A design selecting the 6 m route without relying on a barrier is not selecting the amended reduced-spacing route. The record should identify which route is being assessed before presenting barrier details.

## 4.3. Additional barrier-extent provision

Where the 3 m separation option in EESB-204:2025, Clause 4.3, is used, the fire barrier shall extend **at least 0.5 m beyond the container's height and at least 0.5 m beyond the container's length**.

Both height and length extent shall be addressed in the barrier arrangement. The added extent is not a replacement for the barrier rating and does not permit a separation smaller than the base clause allows.

The design record should show the container envelope and barrier envelope together, so that the additional extent can be understood. Merely labelling an item “fire barrier” does not describe its extent relative to the container.

## 5. Unchanged provisions

| Item | Position after this amendment |
|---|---|
| No barrier relied upon | Minimum separation remains 6 m |
| Barrier rated at least 2 hours | Minimum separation remains 3 m; added extent applies |
| BESS boundary clearance, Clause 4.5 | Minimum remains 10 m |

## 6. Review and citation

For a barrier-based conclusion, cite EESB-204:2025, Clause 4.3, **as amended by A1:2026**. Keep the barrier extent evidence with the rating and layout evidence. Do not describe this amendment as increasing the basic separation distances: its change concerns the barrier envelope only. Earlier supporting notes should be read alongside this additional current provision.
