# Lightning protection for solar and storage sites

| Title block | Detail |
|---|---|
| Document number | EESB-160 |
| Edition | 2024 |
| Status | Current |
| Date | 1 March 2024 |
| Scope | Site lightning assessment, assessment-dependent BESS air terminals and earth-ring bonding |

## 1. Application

This fictional document addresses the record of lightning protection decisions for solar and storage installations. It links the site assessment to the protection arrangement, rather than assuming that the same equipment is appropriate at every location. Its provisions should not be used to infer a project-specific assessment outcome that has not been recorded.

## 2. Site information

The assessment record should identify the site layout and the equipment arrangement reviewed. Solar arrays, storage containers and associated buildings should be recognisable in the supporting information. If equipment positions change, the record should identify whether the assessment relates to the earlier or revised arrangement.

## 3. Design interfaces

The lightning protection record should be coordinated with the equipment layout and the earthing drawing. It should distinguish an air-terminal arrangement from the main earth ring to which the protection system is connected. A reference to one element alone does not describe the complete relationship addressed by this document.

## 4.1. Site assessment

A **lightning risk assessment shall be completed for every site**. The report should identify the assessment and its design basis. The absence of a particular item of protection on a drawing is not enough to determine whether an assessment has been undertaken or what conclusion it reached.

The assessment record should explain the selection of protection measures within its own fictional scope. This extract does not provide an assessment calculation method or assign a generic risk category to all solar and storage developments.

## 5.3. BESS air terminals

Air terminals shall be provided on BESS containers **where the lightning risk assessment requires them**. This is an assessment-dependent requirement. It should not be rewritten as an unconditional statement that every container always needs an air terminal, nor as permission to omit one without reference to the assessment.

The design record should show how the arrangement follows the recorded assessment conclusion. Container identifiers should agree with the equipment schedule so that the evidence can be followed for each relevant item.

## 6.2. Main earth-ring connection

The lightning protection system shall be **bonded to the main earth ring**. Show this interface on the coordinated protection and earthing records. A local symbol without a clear relationship to the main ring should not be treated as a full description of the intended connection.

## 7. Review record

The reviewer should distinguish the assessment, the selected arrangement and the installation evidence. These records answer different questions: why a measure was selected, what was designed and what was observed. Referencing the same drawing for every question can obscure the reasoning.

## 8. Reporting boundaries

This document does not set the earth electrode resistance acceptance limit; that is addressed in EESB-150:2023, Clause 4.2. It also does not replace the two separate BESS bonding points in EESB-150:2023, Clause 4.4. Keep each requirement attached to its own source when preparing the design basis report.
