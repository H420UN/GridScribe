## 1. Draft status

This document is a fictional consultation proposal. It is **not in force and must not be cited as a requirement**. The document is included to support discussion of future design flexibility. Its appearance alongside current standards in a library does not change its status or make its proposed values mandatory.

## 2. Current acceptance basis

EESB-305:2022 remains the current standard. Its Clause 6.4 specifies 10 m between rapid chargers and any BESS container. A present assessment should use the current edition for the compliance conclusion, while describing this draft separately if the proposal is relevant to future layout decisions.

The draft is not evidence that the earlier requirement has been withdrawn. A later year in a document identifier is insufficient to establish precedence: status and effective date matter as well.

## 3. Consultation purpose

The proposal invites consideration of an increased spatial allowance at combined charging and storage sites. Comments may discuss practical layout consequences, redevelopment constraints and how a proposed change could affect future phases. No adoption date is established by this draft, and the consultation closing date is not a date on which the proposal automatically becomes a requirement.

## 4. Review boundaries

This consultation does not assess any particular project. A site may satisfy the current edition while having less space than the proposal describes. That difference is a future exposure to consider, not a present failure against this document. Any voluntary reservation of additional space should be identified as a project choice.

## 5. Recording a response

A response should distinguish the proposal under discussion from the requirements already in force. Cite the draft identifier and proposed clause number when discussing the proposal, and the current edition when stating the acceptance criterion. Mixing the two can make an otherwise clear review misleading.

## 6.4. Proposed rapid-charger separation

**Proposed only:** rapid chargers would be at least **15 m from BESS containers**. This proposed Clause 6.4 is advisory during consultation and is not a current requirement.

The proposal should not be described using an unqualified statement that chargers “must be 15 m away”. Where it is mentioned in a report, retain the words “draft”, “proposed” and “not in force” close to the distance.

## 7. Design-risk discussion

Teams may consider reserving space where the cost is small and the arrangement remains practical. Such a decision can preserve options without asserting that the final publication will contain the same proposal. The consultation may result in revision, adoption or a different outcome; this document does not predict which.

## 8. Closing and follow-up

Consultation closes **31 January 2027**. Before any later design release, verify the published status rather than assuming the draft has advanced. Until a replacement is brought into force, record current compliance against EESB-305:2022 and maintain this consultation as a separately labelled risk.
