# Cable installation and trenching

| Title block | Detail |
|---|---|
| Document number | EESB-110 |
| Edition | 2023 |
| Status | Current |
| Date | 1 April 2023 |
| Scope | Buried LV and 33 kV cable routes, warning tape and shared-trench separation |

## 1. Application

This fictional document addresses the physical arrangement of buried cable routes at solar, storage and charging sites. It is concerned with installation geometry rather than the calculation of cable voltage drop. A route can have adequate burial depth while its electrical sizing still requires a separate calculation.

## 2. Route information

The route drawing should identify the cable service, nominal voltage category and the sections sharing a trench. A schedule reference should connect the civil route to the electrical circuit identifier. Where a route changes direction or passes between equipment zones, the record should remain clear about which cables are present.

The design record should distinguish a general route corridor from a detailed trench section. A corridor on a site plan does not demonstrate the vertical arrangement of the cables beneath it.

## 3.4. Minimum burial depth

The minimum burial depth shall be **600 mm for LV cables** and **800 mm for 33 kV cables**. The cable category should be stated with the recorded depth. A single depth value without a cable category cannot demonstrate which requirement has been checked.

The installation record should describe the relevant trench section and its relationship to the route drawing. This makes later review possible without assuming that all sections have an identical arrangement.

## 3.6. Warning tape

Warning tape shall be installed **150 mm above buried cables**. Record its vertical relationship to the cable position, not only its presence somewhere within the trench. A drawing annotation should be consistent with the section detail and with the installation inspection.

The warning tape provision is distinct from burial depth. Compliance with one dimension should not be used as evidence for the other when completing an inspection record.

## 4.2. LV and HV shared trenches

There shall be at least **300 mm separation between LV and HV cables in shared trenches**. The record should identify the shared section and the cable groups to which the separation applies. This separation is a cable-installation dimension, not an equipment separation for BESS containers or charging units.

Where separate routes are selected, the drawing should make that arrangement understandable rather than leaving a reviewer to infer that a shared trench exists.

## 5. Construction interface

Route changes should be transferred to the record drawing so that the installed arrangement can be compared with the design. Distinguish an intended section from an inspected section. A revised route should also be passed back to the electrical designer because changed length can affect calculations outside the scope of this document.

## 6. Completion record

The close-out record should group each observed dimension with the route identifier, cable category and relevant clause. EESB-101:2024 remains the source for AC voltage-drop limits and ambient factors. Quoting a compliant trench section does not replace that electrical check, and quoting an acceptable voltage drop does not demonstrate the burial arrangement.
