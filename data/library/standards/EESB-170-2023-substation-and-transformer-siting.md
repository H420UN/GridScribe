# Substation and transformer siting

| Title block | Detail |
|---|---|
| Document number | EESB-170 |
| Edition | 2023 |
| Status | Current |
| Date | 1 June 2023 |
| Scope | Transformer boundary clearance and oil containment at fictional energy-site substations |

## 1. Application

This document addresses the siting of transformers and their containment arrangements. Its boundary clearance applies to transformers, not BESS containers. Both kinds of equipment can appear on one site drawing, so the equipment subject should be stated whenever a clearance is reported.

## 2. Layout information

The substation layout should identify the transformer position, site boundary and associated containment arrangement. Use equipment identifiers consistent with the single-line diagram and equipment schedule. A reference to the substation zone alone may not identify the transformer position within that zone.

The design record should distinguish the site boundary from an internal fence or the outline of an equipment platform. The label used for the measurement should remain consistent between the site description and the detailed layout.

## 3. Review boundaries

The assessment should state which transformer is being reviewed and the relevant boundary relationship. This document does not establish a general separation for every item within a substation. A conclusion about transformer clearance should not be transferred to nearby storage equipment or charging equipment.

## 4.2. Transformer boundary clearance

Transformers shall be at least **5 m from the site boundary**. Record the design distance together with the equipment identifier and boundary location. The acceptance statement should make clear that this is a transformer check.

The 5 m figure is **not the BESS boundary requirement**. For BESS containers, consult EESB-204:2025, Clause 4.5, which specifies 10 m. Using the transformer figure for storage containers would apply a requirement to the wrong equipment category, even though both sources concern site boundaries.

## 4.4. Oil containment

The transformer oil containment bund shall be sized for **110% of the transformer oil volume**. The record should identify the transformer oil volume used for the comparison and the containment provision associated with that transformer.

The requirement is expressed relative to the relevant oil volume. An equipment rating or the dimensions of a transformer platform do not independently establish the containment capacity. Keep the containment calculation separate from the boundary-clearance measurement.

## 5. Design coordination

If a transformer is relocated, review both its clearance and the associated containment arrangement. A moved equipment symbol should not leave the containment record describing the previous location. The coordinated layout should be understandable without relying on informal recollection of the change.

## 6. Evidence and reporting

Use the layout for the recorded distance and the containment record for the volume comparison. A general statement that the substation is compliant should identify which checks support that conclusion. Where this document is used in a wider design basis report, retain the document number, edition and clause so the reader can distinguish it from the BESS siting standard.

## 7. Scope boundary

Cable voltage drop, EV bay dimensions and storage-container separation are outside this document. Their presence within the same development does not extend the transformer requirements to those subjects.
