# BESS siting and fire separation

| Title block | Detail |
|---|---|
| Document number | EESB-204 |
| Edition | 2025 |
| Status | Current; read with A1:2026 |
| Date | In force from 1 March 2025 |
| Scope | BESS container separation, boundary clearance and container gas detection |

## 1. Application

This edition replaces EESB-204:2021 from 1 March 2025. It establishes the fictional current siting basis for a BESS installation. Earlier technical notes and case studies may describe arrangements accepted under the former edition. Those descriptions do not provide an alternative compliance route for a current assessment.

## 2. Edition and amendment control

Use the edition identifier with each clause citation. Amendment 1, issued 1 June 2026 as EESB-204:2025 A1:2026, adds detail to the barrier option in Clause 4.3. It does not replace this document as a whole. A review of a barrier arrangement should therefore retain both the base clause and the amendment reference.

## 3. Layout information

The layout record should identify each container, the separations between containers, the site boundary and any proposed barrier. Record whether a barrier is absent, proposed or installed. An unbuilt option should not be described as an existing feature merely because it appears in a discussion of remedial actions.

## 4.3. Container separation

The minimum separation between BESS containers shall be **6 m**, or **3 m where a fire barrier rated for at least 2 hours is installed between them**.

The reduced-distance option depends on the barrier, not simply on a statement that the compound is compact. Where no barrier is provided, the 6 m minimum applies. When the barrier option is selected, also apply the extent provisions added by EESB-204:2025 A1:2026 to this clause.

## 4.5. Site boundary

BESS containers shall be at least **10 m from the site boundary**. This requirement concerns BESS containers, not the transformer position. A clearance specified for a different item of equipment is not a substitute for this clause.

## 5.1. Gas detection

Gas detection shall be provided in **every BESS container**. The equipment and commissioning records should identify the provision by container, rather than describing only a general compound system. The layout assessment and the detection assessment should remain separately traceable in the design record.

## 6. Changes from the 2021 edition

| Subject | EESB-204:2021 | EESB-204:2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Clause 4.3, container separation | 3 m minimum | 6 m, or 3 m with a barrier rated at least 2 hours |
| Clause 4.5, boundary clearance | 8 m minimum | 10 m minimum |
| Status from 1 March 2025 | Superseded | Current |

## 7. Review conclusion

State the selected spacing route and the evidence supporting it. Do not blend the former separation with the current boundary clause and present the combination as one edition. If a design changes to the barrier option, record the barrier rating and amendment extent check alongside the revised layout. A compliant boundary clearance alone does not demonstrate compliant internal spacing.
