# BESS ventilation and thermal management

| Title block | Detail |
|---|---|
| Document number | EESB-210 |
| Edition | 2024 |
| Status | Current |
| Date | 1 August 2024 |
| Scope | Battery-cell temperature control and orientation of deflagration vent discharge |

## 1. Application

This fictional document addresses thermal management inside BESS containers and the relationship between container vents and circulation routes. Battery-cell temperature is a different design quantity from the ambient temperature used to derate a cable in an inverter room. The two values should not be substituted for one another.

## 2. Equipment information

The design record should identify the container arrangement and the thermal management package serving it. Container identifiers should agree with the equipment schedule. A combined plant description should still make clear how each container is covered by the intended arrangement.

The relevant information includes the location of walkways and access routes around the compound. A vent orientation shown without the surrounding route layout can be difficult to interpret.

## 3. Design interfaces

The thermal management description should distinguish cell conditions from room or outdoor conditions. Where different temperature figures appear in a report, label the subject of each figure. A cable ambient assumption is not evidence that the battery cells will be maintained within their required range.

## 4. Functional record

The equipment description should explain the intended relationship between the battery system and its HVAC provision. This extract does not prescribe an additional ventilation rate or a separate temperature criterion for other equipment. Avoid extending the cell-temperature requirement to unrelated site spaces.

## 5.2. Battery-cell temperature

The HVAC system shall keep battery cells between **15 °C and 30 °C**. The design record should state that this is a cell-temperature range. It should not describe it merely as a general site ambient condition.

Where thermal performance evidence is summarised, retain its relationship to the relevant equipment arrangement. A generic product description should not be silently relabelled as a site measurement.

## 6.3. Vent discharge orientation

Deflagration vents shall **discharge away from walkways and access routes**. The layout should show both the vent direction and the circulation routes to which the requirement applies. A direction arrow without a corresponding route arrangement does not provide a complete explanation.

The review should distinguish the operating vent orientation from a temporary transport or storage arrangement. The equipment position used in the review should match the project layout revision being assessed.

## 7. Coordination with siting

This document does not establish BESS-to-BESS separation or boundary clearance. Those matters are addressed by EESB-204:2025 and its applicable amendment. An acceptable thermal arrangement does not remove a separate siting requirement, and a compliant siting distance does not demonstrate the cell-temperature range.

## 8. Reporting

State thermal management and vent orientation as separate matters with their respective clause references. If an equipment arrangement is revised, update the supporting description rather than copying the previous conclusion without review. Keep evidence of intended provision distinct from observations made during commissioning, so that a reader understands the basis of each statement.
