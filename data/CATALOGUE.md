## 1. Upload boundary

Upload only the files inside `data/library/`. This catalogue sits outside that folder and is not part of the 36-document index. Filenames are unique across the three library folders. All publications, standards, projects, clients and author roles in the library are fictional training material.

## 2. Status labels

The status column uses the catalogue categories **Current**, **Superseded**, **Draft**, **Amendment**, **WSP note** and **Project**. “Amendment” identifies the current amendment to its base standard. “WSP note” includes supporting notes, the historic case study and the review checklist; it does not confer standards authority. The linked title blocks and status warnings provide the detailed context.
